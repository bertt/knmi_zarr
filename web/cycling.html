👋 About this site

This page finds the nearest KNMI weather station and turns its latest measurements into practical advice for cyclists: a ride comfort score, a training score, what to wear, whether you need a rain jacket, and how to clean your bike afterwards. It also warns about ice, fog, darkness and thunder.

It uses 18 weather parameters: temperature, dew point, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction and gusts, sun radiation, visibility, cloud cover, sunshine duration, rain intensity, rain over the last 1, 6 and 24 hours, and the present-weather code.

🧊 Data and Zarr

The observations are 10-minute KNMI measurements (dataset, CC BY 4.0), published as NetCDF and converted to Zarr. Not an official KNMI product.

Zarr is an open format for chunked, compressed multi-dimensional arrays. Because the data is split into small files, your browser can fetch just the pieces it needs straight from static storage, with no server or API in between.
