👋 About this site

This page finds the nearest KNMI weather station and turns its latest measurements into practical advice for cyclists: a ride score, what to wear, whether you need a rain jacket, and how to clean your bike afterwards. It also warns about ice, fog, darkness and thunder.

It uses 18 weather parameters: temperature, dew point, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction and gusts, sun radiation, visibility, cloud cover, sunshine duration, rain intensity, rain over the last 1, 6 and 24 hours, and the present-weather code.

Next to the temperature on the bike, the 🧊 pill shows a standard "feels like" temperature from air temperature and wind: the wind chill formula (T ≤ 10 °C, wind > 4.8 km/h) 13.12 + 0.6215·T − 11.37·v0.16 + 0.3965·T·v0.16 (v = wind at 10 m in km/h), or the heat index (T ≥ 27 °C, with humidity). It is hidden when neither formula applies (e.g. calm wind, or 10–27 °C). It does not include riding speed, darkness or wind direction, so it differs from the temperature on the bike.

Cycling quotes come from The Rules by Velominati.

🧊 Data and GeoParquet

The observations are 10-minute KNMI measurements (dataset, CC BY 4.0), published as NetCDF and converted to GeoParquet. Not an official KNMI product.

GeoParquet is an open, columnar format for geospatial data. The stations are sorted by location and stored in small row groups, so your browser can fetch just the pieces it needs (HTTP range requests) straight from static storage, with no server or API in between.
